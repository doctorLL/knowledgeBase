## 重点单词

### 第一梯队

**souvenir** /ˌsuːvəˈnɪr/ n. 纪念品
- `A souvenir is something you buy to help you remember a place you visited.` / `I always buy a magnet as a souvenir.`
- 旅途中买来留念的小东西:磁铁、明信片、钥匙扣、T 恤都算
- ⚠️ 重音在最后一个音节 -nir,前面的 sou- 很轻,别读成三个音节平均;别和 memory 混 —— souvenir 是"买来的实物",memory 是"记忆"

**hiking** /ˈhaɪkɪŋ/ n. 徒步、远足(动词 go hiking)
- `Hiking means walking in nature for fun or exercise.` / `You need strong, comfortable shoes. We call them hiking boots.`
- 指"去山里/森林/河边走",不是城里散步;城里公园散步叫 a walk
- ⚠️ 鞋叫 hiking boots,不是 hiking shoes;动词原形 hike,`go for a hike`

**spontaneous** /spɑːnˈteɪniəs/ adj. 随性的、即兴的
- `Spontaneous means you do something without a lot of planning. You decide to do it suddenly, at the moment.` / `I am the opposite of spontaneous. I am a planner.`
- 反义:planned / organized。a spontaneous trip = 说走就走的旅行
- ⚠️ 重音在第二个音节 -ta-,本集里 Emma 一开始把它口误读成 "spun-taneous",这正好是听力点 —— 先抓"计划 vs 不计划"的语境再猜

**dream vacation** /driːm vəˈkeɪʃn/ n. 梦想中的度假
- `A dream vacation is the vacation you want most in the world.` / `My dream vacation is Brazil.`
- dream 作名词修饰 vacation,美国英语里不说 dream holiday
- ⚠️ 问法是 `What is your dream vacation?`,回答直接接地点

**local** /ˈloʊkl/ adj. 当地的
- `Local means from this place, from this area.` / `If you are lost, you can ask a local person for directions.`
- local food(当地食物)、local restaurant、local person;也可作名词 a local = 当地人
- ⚠️ 和 "place" 无关,别写成 loccal;重音第一音节

## 高频句型

| 功能 | 句子 |

| 问度假带什么 | What do you always take with you on vacation? |

| 问梦想度假 | What is your dream vacation? |
| 问长还是短 | Do you like long vacations or short vacations? |
| 问怎么去 | How do you travel to your vacations? By plane? By train? By car? |

| 问最美的地方 | What is the most beautiful place you visited? |

两个特别地道、单独记:

- **I'll have that, please.** —— 旅行点餐的万能句,不认识菜名时指着菜单说"我就要这个",比 I want this 更地道。
- **What is your dream vacation?** —— dream 直接作名词修饰 vacation,问"你最想去的度假"。

## 过去式与语法要点

### 过去式清单(聊上次度假时真实出现)

| 原形 | 过去式 | 原形 | 过去式 |
|---|---|---|---|
| visit | visited | stay | stayed |
| cook | cooked | watch | watched |
| go | went | eat | ate |
| swim | swam | get | got |
| lose | lost | have | had |
| be | was | book | booked |

总结里原话:`We use the past tense a lot, like I visited, I swam, I ate.`

### 语法要点(视频里真实出现)

- **过去时(聊上次度假)**:`My last vacation was in Canada. I visited my cousin.` —— 一讲过去的旅行就全程用过去时。
- **将来时两种**:`I am planning to go to Spain`(进行时表计划)vs `I want to see Tokyo`(want to 表愿望)。总结里原话点明了这两种。
- **have to 表"不得不"**:过去式 had to。`I had to wait at the airport for twelve hours.` / `I had to buy new clothes.`
- **Do you ever...?**:问"你曾经/有没有过",`Do you ever have a bad vacation?`

## 听力难点

- `spontaneous` —— 本集 Emma 一开始口误读成 "spun-taneous",Jack 跟着卡壳;听到卡壳别慌,先抓"计划 vs 不计划"的语境
- `souvenir` —— 重音在最后 /ˌsuːvəˈnɪr/,前面的 sou- 很轻,容易被听成 "souver" 或整个漏掉
- `poutine` /puːˈtiːn/ —— 加拿大食物,法语借词,第一次听到几乎肯定懵,靠后面解释 french fries with cheese curds 猜
- `fjord` /fjɔːrd/ —— 少见词,靠 "Norway + water between mountains" 猜
- `takeoff` —— 连读成 /teɪkɔːf/,和 take off(脱下)同源,容易听成两个词
- `I'll have that, please` —— have 弱读,整句像 /aɪl hæv ðæt/,点餐场景要能反应过来
- `my flight was cancelled` —— was 弱读 + 被动,过去时取消航班;cancelled 双 l
