## 重点单词

### 第一梯队

**temperature** /ˈtemprətʃər/ n. 温度
- `Temperature tells us how hot or cold it is.` / `Today it is 22 degrees Celsius.`
- 问几度用 What is the temperature? 答 `It is 22 degrees.`
- ⚠️ 重音在第一音节 TEM-,不是 tem-PER-;几度用 **degrees**(复数),不说 degree

**forecast** /ˈfɔːrkæst/ n. 天气预报(也可作动词,过去式 forecast)
- `The weather forecast tells us if it will be sunny or rainy tomorrow.` / `I check the forecast every day.`
- weather forecast 常简说成 the forecast
- ⚠️ 重音在 FOR,不是 fore-CAST;作动词过去式不变形

**humid** /ˈhjuːmɪd/ adj. 潮湿的(空气里水分多)
- `Humid means there is a lot of water in the air.` / `Their weather is always very humid.`
- 和 wet 不同:humid 指"空气潮",wet 指"沾了水、淋湿了"。天气潮用 humid,淋湿用 wet
- 名词:humidity,`The humidity is very high today.`

**drizzle** /ˈdrɪzl/ n./v. 毛毛雨
- `Drizzle is not heavy. The water drops are very small.` / `It's not raining hard, it's just drizzling.`
- 不是"小雨"的正式说法,特指"很细很小的雨";动词 it's drizzling
- ⚠️ 和 rain 不是一回事,程度最轻;反义是 downpour

**downpour** /ˈdaʊnpɔːr/ n. 倾盆大雨
- `A downpour is very, very heavy rain. A lot of rain falls in a short time.`
- 突然的暴雨,`there was a downpour` 用 a
- ⚠️ 重音在 DOWN;drizzle(轻)和 downpour(重)是两极

**breeze** /briːz/ n. 微风
- `A breeze is a light wind. It is pleasant.` / `There's a lovely breeze.`
- 舒服的微风;wind 可大可小,大风 dangerous
- ⚠️ 形容词 breezy 形容"有微风的":a breezy day

## 高频句型

| 功能 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 问天气 | What's the weather like? / What's the weather like today? |
| 问将来天气 | What will the weather be like tomorrow? / Is it going to rain tomorrow? |
| 描述天气 | It's sunny / rainy / windy / snowy / cloudy / foggy. |
| 描述温度 | It's hot / warm / cool / cold. |
| 说今天 | It's a warm, sunny day. |
| 报温度 | It is 22 degrees Celsius. |
| 看预报 | The weather forecast says it will rain. |
| 问是否下雨 | Is it raining? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. |
| 聊感受 | That sounds lovely. / That sounds cozy and comfortable. |
| 搭讪开场 | Lovely day, isn't it? / What a rainy day! |

两个特别地道、单独记:

- **Lovely day, isn't it?** —— 英式搭讪天气的万能开场(反义疑问句,尾音上扬)
- **What a rainy day!** —— What + a + 名词感叹句,比 It's rainy 更有情绪

## 过去式与语法要点

### 过去式清单(上周天气回顾里真实出现)

| 原形 | 过去式 | 原形 | 过去式 |
|---|---|---|---|
| be | was | become | became |
| go | went | start | started |
| fly | flew | check | checked |
| drink | drank | know | knew |
| stay | stayed | have to | had to |

`My hat flew off in the wind. I had to run after it.` —— flew 是 fly 的过去式,听力里最容易漏。

### 语法要点(视频里真实出现)

- **反义疑问句**:`Lovely day, isn't it?` —— 陈述 + 反义小尾巴,期待对方附和,是天气搭讪的标准句式。
- **感叹句**:`What a rainy day!` / `So windy!` —— What + a + 名词,或直接 So + 形容词。
- **将来时两种问法**:`What will the weather be like tomorrow?`(will)vs `Is it going to rain tomorrow?`(be going to,更口语)。
- **it 指天气**:英语里天气主语一律用 it —— `It's sunny.`,不说 "The weather is sunny."

## 听力难点

- `What's the weather like?` —— 三个词连读成 /wʌts ðə ˈweðər laɪk/,中间的 like 容易被听成 "light"
- `isn't it?` —— 反义疑问句尾音弱读,几乎只剩 /ˈɪznt ɪt/,但整句意思靠它
- `22 degrees` —— 数字 + degrees 连读,容易漏 2
- `drizzle` 和 `downpour` —— 都是"雨",一轻一重,听力里靠后面解释区分
- `Lovely day, isn't it?` —— lovely 重音在前,day 拖长,整句像打招呼而不是问句
- 否定两种都出现:`No, it isn't.` 和 `No, it's not.` —— isn't 缩读成 /ˈɪznt/
