## 重点单词

### 第一梯队

**eventful** /ɪˈventfəl/ adj. 多事的、内容丰富的
- `My weekend was really nice. It was eventful.` —— `It means many things happened.`
- 比 busy 更进一步:busy 是"忙",eventful 是"发生了很多事",可以是好事
- 搭配:an eventful day / an eventful year

**relaxing** /rɪˈlæksɪŋ/ adj. 令人放松的
- `My weekend was very relaxing.` / `I listen to relaxing music.`
- 反义:stressful、busy
- ⚠️ relaxing 形容**事**,relaxed 形容**人**。`I felt relaxed.` ✓ / `I felt relaxing.` ✗

**brunch** /brʌntʃ/ n. 早午餐
- `It was breakfast and lunch together. Brunch!`
- breakfast + lunch,一般上午 10 点到 12 点吃
- 搭配:have brunch / go for brunch on Sunday

**movie marathon** /ˈmuːvi ˈmærəθən/ n. 电影马拉松
- `I watched a trilogy of fantasy movies.` → `It was a movie marathon.`
- marathon 原指 42 公里长跑 → 引申为"一口气做很久的事"
- 同类:TV show marathon、binge watching(刷剧)

**rested** /ˈrestɪd/ adj. 休息好了的
- `I feel very rested now. I'm ready for the week.`
- 动词 rest → 形容词 rested;常用 feel rested / well-rested
- 反义:tired、exhausted

**delicious** /dɪˈlɪʃəs/ adj. 好吃的
- `The green curry I ordered was delicious.`
- ⚠️ 只形容食物和饮料,不能说 a delicious movie(本集专门拿这个开玩笑)
- 形容影视用:good / funny / exciting / moving

**sentence** /ˈsentəns/ n.句子，句；判决，判刑；命题  v.判决，宣判，判刑
- `Sentence is the basic unit of speech communication.`

## 高频句型

| 功能 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 问周末 | How was your weekend? |
| 答(好) | It was great / really nice / a lot of fun. |
| 答(忙) | It was busy. I had a lot of things to do. |
| 答(闲) | It was quiet and relaxing. |
| 问细节 | What did you do on Saturday? / Did you go out? / Did you see any friends? |
| 说感受 | That sounds lovely. / That sounds so peaceful. |
| 表达羡慕 | I'm a little jealous. |
| 惊叹 | No wonder you're tired. |
| 总结 | The important thing is to rest and do things you enjoy. |

两个特别地道、单独记:

- **It was a good tired.** —— 累但是累得值,中文没有对应说法
- **You know me too well.** —— 你太了解我了

## 复述练习

**① 60 秒版**:用 5 句话讲完 Emma 的周六,不看原文。
提示词:coffee → supermarket → park → cinema → pizza

**② 替换版**:把 Emma / Jack 换成你自己和朋友,套同样结构。
`Last Saturday I ... Then in the afternoon I ... In the evening I ...`

**③ 挖空版**(先听 01:16–04:30 再填)

> On Saturday morning, I ______ up early. I ______ my friend Sarah for coffee. We ______ to a new cafe downtown. After that, I ______ to the supermarket. In the afternoon, I ______ for a walk in the park. In the evening, I ______ to the cinema and I ______ a comedy. After the movie, we ______ hungry, so we ______ to a pizza restaurant.

答案:woke / met / went / went / went / went / saw / were / went

## 听力难点

- `No wonder you're tired.` —— no wonder 连读成 /noʊ ˈwʌndər/,后面 you're 几乎吞掉
- `11 o'clock` —— 慢速播客里数字反而容易漏
- 比分 `2-1` 读作 **two one**,不是 "two to one"
- `I didn't leave my apartment all day.` —— 否定 + all day 常被听成 "I did leave"

用 3 个本课新词回答 **What did you do last weekend?**,写 5~8 句。
